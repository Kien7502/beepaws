## Priority Issues
- **[P0] Zero real imagery.** Hero full-bleed scene, §3 outcome image, §4 product photo, 3× §6 customer photos are all labeled placeholder divs. The design is image-led (scrim over a scene); without photos the hero renders as a cocoa gradient over flat honey-tint. Fix: land the photo pipeline, or design an honest interim non-photo hero. 
- **[P1] §6 proof ships placeholder quotes + "Name" attribution** and a visible "Placeholder proof" disclaimer; also re-introduces the uniform 3-card grid. Fix: one real testimonial beats three placeholders; vary crop/size when real photos land.
- **[P2] Bark small-print `#A8A48F` at text-xs ≈ 3.9:1 → fails AA** (needs 4.5). Bump toward cream. `#CFCBBA` body passes (~6.0:1).
- **[P2] Homepage close = same seal-on-paper card as PDP FinalCTA** — brand echo vs one-note close; consider differentiating one of them.
- **[P3] Hardcoded hex on bark band; dead gradient-text + purple focus ring in globals; `?variant`/`?ground` decision debt keeps page dynamic.**
